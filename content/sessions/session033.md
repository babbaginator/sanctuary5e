# Episode 33 | Fire on the Water
As soon as the combat ended, Rua used Divine Sense and detected an undead presence down in the ship’s ballast. Splitting up to secure what we could, Sher and Tuor headed to the lower deck while Aucha rushed into the captain’s cabin to grab the books and the chest. Inside, Aucha noticed something unusual about the captain’s desk. After locating and pressing a hidden button, a secret drawer popped open, revealing a stack of letters. She collected the letters alongside the books and the chest. Realizing the caskets in the hold were far too heavy to carry by hand, Valross and Aucha searched the main deck and found a pulley system to hoist one up. They decided to take only a single casket for now. While hauling it out, the burning mast collapsed, injuring them both, though they managed to drag the casket onto the deck through their effort. 

Meanwhile, on the lower deck, Tuor attempted to smash open the storage room door, only managing to leave a scratch on the wood. Unimpressed, Sher stepped in and picked the lock with her thieves' tools. The moment the door swung open, Rua bolted past them toward the bottom deck. Inside the storage, Sher retrieved the caged bird hanging from the ceiling - a beautiful blue parrot, and unlocked one of the treasure chests, discovering smaller boxes packed with large, dark brown pills. She pocketed three boxes containing twenty pills each. Down below, Rua reached the locked hatch leading to the ballast, ripped the lock open, and dropped inside. There, he confronted a terrifying undead creature, a Bodak and managed to overpower and destroy it. 

As we all regrouped on the main deck, we heard people on the pier trying to shove the burning vessel away from the docks. Rua immediately summoned his steed, Talos, leaped onto the pier, and scanned the area for suspicious figures, though he spotted no one. Kori-el also escaped the deck using Misty Step. To transport the heavy casket, Tuor summoned his steed, Kiwi, in the form of a fey elk. We used the Rope of Climbing to lash the casket securely onto Kiwi’s back, sat Valross on top, and handed him the parrot’s cage. Tuor commanded Kiwi to Fey Step onto the pier, and then he and Aucha used Misty Step to clear the burning ship just as a group of mages arrived and used wind magic to push the vessel out to sea. 

Meanwhile, Sher shifted into a pool of blood, slipped into the water and eventually met back up with Rua. We decided to split into two groups for the time being, with Aucha relaying the plan to regroup at the Temple of Boccob to the others. Rua also apologized to Sher for his impulsive actions on the ship, admitting he had let his emotions get the better of him, and Sher forgave him. Aucha, Tuor, Valross, and Kori-el took the direct route toward the temple, cutting through the Field of Heroes. To avoid drawing attention, we put blankets over the casket on Kiwi’s back and posed as a troupe of traveling entertainers heading to the Temple of Elonce, the goddess of art, for her blessing and loudly advertising our show as we went. A 25-gold-piece bribe got us past the guards at the Field of Heroes with minimal questioning. 

The guards at the High Road entrance were far stricter, though they ultimately let us pass after offering advice on Elonce’s worship and practices. As Sher and Rua crossed Tosser’s Square, the former noticed street urchins darting into alleyways the moment they laid eyes on Rua, prompting her to warn him. They crossed into Riverglen without further incident. Along the way, Rua confided in Sher that he had learned his mother was killed by the Burning Hand, the arsonist group with ties to the Crimson Brotherhood, explaining his rage aboard the ship. In turn, Sher opened up about her lover, Valerie, who had helped her escape the Crimson Brotherhood and was murdered by them for it.
