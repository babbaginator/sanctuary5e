# Sir Karon Rakmar

## Description
Sir Karon is a distinguished human nobleman in his mid-to-late fifties with a proud face, sharp features, and a full head of silver hair. In public, he presents himself as a socially active paladin of Selune, but privately he is a shrewd, vain opportunist who utilizes blackmail, coercion, and doppelganger spies. Driven by ambition and fear of losing status, he needs secret power, collaborating in the sewers with the 
Crimson Brotherhood and The Scour on lycanthropic-necromancy experiments. He is notoriously seen at court masquerades with exotic, dark-haired foreign companions who are never seen again.

## Notes
The party has not met him.
