# Aucha Mondegel
**Gnome Warlock (Archfey Patron, Oak Princess)**
## Description
Aucha wears a suit of magical barbarian studded leather armor (Studly Leather) which gives her an unnaturally "buff" physical appearance. She sometimes disguises herself as a dwarf and poses as an exchange student from Ostegar. She is frequently accompanied by her rat familiar Tilly and also has befriended the rats living in her Hat of Vermin.

## Personality
* **Scholar of Cataclysms**: Driven since childhood by a deep curiosity regarding ancient magical disasters, lost lore, and historical cataclysms
* **Compassionate & Animal-Kin**: Demonstrates strong empathy toward creatures, rescuing trapped squirrels like Chittner, conversing with wild cats and mice, nd relying on Tilly for scouting
* **Resourceful & Crafty**: Shows signature gnomish ingenuity—such as forging lead molds for divine amulets, transforming into gas to scout enemy barracks, and using Mage Hand with illusions to trick explosive scrying devices
* **Fiercely Loyal**: Though occasionally hotheaded in high-stakes situations, she stands firmly by her companions, warmly reassuring Sher about her snake-like features when others recoil and using her warlock abilities to protect her squad

## Background
Accounts of ancient magical cataclysms have fascinated Aucha from her childhood, and while on occasional recreational hikes in Eldergleam, she was sometimes overheard by the Oak Princess (fey noble) talking to herself about it, she was overheard (maybe multiple times) by the Oak Princess, who later approached Aucha and offered some magical powers to help her in her quest. Aucha owes her a favour in the future, as well as serving as somewhat of a missionary for fey in non-fey lands. She originally got powers and left home because she wanted to learn about the cataclism that destroyed gnomes & elves original home, and prevent a potential new one (that she saw in dreams)

## Key Events & Accomplishments
* **The Archfey Pact**: Contracted with the Oak Princess in Eldergleam to gain power to study and prevent future cataclysms, suffering the tragic cost of having her family completely forget she exists
* **The Blight & Extraplanar Base**: Helped defeat the boney juggernaut and ice toad in the Norwind mines, infiltrated the extraplanar base by imitating Zog ("Ape in a Cape"), turned into a cloud of gas to scout enemy positions, and helped slay three extraplanar "Masters" (including a Mindflayer) before escaping an exploding vessel via teleporter to Ostegar.
* **Rescuing Captives & Fey Contracts**: Saved kidnapped merchants (Albo, Suzanne, and Halfred) from rogue bandits near Gimble, paid off a Shadow Fey of the Court of Roots by letting it extract the memory of a story, and eliminated a band of rogue Redcaps and a quickling in the Eldergreen swamp.
* **Tarbalak & The Fey Quest**: Spoke with Tarbalak (The Most Ancient of Trees) and her patron, receiving a divine quest to locate and destroy a corrupted fey sapling in Sanctuary being harvested by the Crimson Brotherhood to forge interdimensional travel keys.
* **Sanctuary Infiltration**: Used forged mining guild papers provided by Tuor to pass city guard inspection, researched Badwater records at the Infinite Archive and Temple of Boccob, turned her team invisible to raid the Crimson Brotherhood's black ship at the Waterfront, uncovered secret letters in the captain's desk, and hauled a runic casket off the burning ship.
## Connections
### Party Members
* **Ruazoz (Rua)**: An Orc Paladin of Gom and Aucha's closest confidant. She shared the painful secret of her warlock pact and forgotten family with him first, and both experienced a divine vision together while drinking dwarven holy spirits.
* **Sher (Rhyza)**: A Yuan-ti Sorcerer. Aucha reassured Sher of her beauty when she revealed her scales and fangs, working closely with her on disguises and magical bluffs.
* **Tuor Earthborn**: A Dwarf Paladin of the Ancients. Tuor provided forged official seals from his father's mining guild for Aucha's party alibi and pledged to write a grand chronicle of their adventures so Aucha's name is remembered.
* **Valros Silverspirit**: A Halfling Rogue. Valros was initially skeptical of Aucha talking to animals and fey, but gained deep respect for her after her warlock quick-thinking saved the party from a Shadow Fey attack.
### NPCs
* **The Oak Princess**: A High Fae noble patron residing between the Court of Branches and Court of Roots who guides Aucha's magical path.
* **The Targanils**: An established gnomish clan in Ostegar and Sanctuary with whom Aucha maintains personal ties.

## Goals & Objectives
* **Prevent a Second Cataclysm (Long-Term)**: Understand the ancient cataclysm that destroyed the original homeland of gnomes and elves, driven by prophetic dreams of a similar disaster threatening the current realm
* **Destroy the Corrupted Fey Sapling (Mid-Term)**: Fulfill her patron's mandate to locate and destroy the corrupted sapling in Sanctuary, returning a living cutting to Tarbalak
* **Thwart the Crimson Brotherhood (Short-Term)**: Investigate their underground operations in the Badwater sewers, decrypt the captain's letters, and protect Sher from Brotherhood assassins.
* **Access Boccob's Deep Vaults**: Return to High Priest Galdrin Goodtree with a rare piece of knowledge to gain entry to restricted vaults containing historical records on the Island of Rhust
