# Aucha Mondegel
## Description
Gnome Warlock (Archfey Patron, Oak Princess)
## Background
Accounts of ancient magical cataclysms have fascinated Aucha from her childhood, and while on occasional recreational hikes in Eldergleam, she was sometimes overheard by the Oak Princess (fey noble) talking to herself about it, she was overheard (maybe multiple times) by the Oak Princess, who later approached Aucha and offered some magical powers to help her in her quest. Aucha owes her a favour in the future, as well as serving as somewhat of a missionary for fey in non-fey lands. 

She originally got powers and left home because she wanted to learn about the cataclism that destroyed gnomes & elves original home, and prevent a potential new one (that she saw in dreams) Would there be an opportunity in Ostegar or later to learn about it?

She could find other people knowledgeable about the earlier cataclysm in Ostegar. There may also be clues in Ostegar (the presence of some strange cultists, some odd sightings in the area, rumors about the ancient ruins beneath Ostegar, etc.) We can certainly build on that.

## Connections
* Aucha knows the Taganils, an old gnomish clan who are patrons of the arts, support training programs and public education, and specialize in the gathering of information and diplomacy.
