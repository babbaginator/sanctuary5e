# The Bottomless Bag of Pungent Cheeses

## Description
A small leather pouch that opens into a magical storage void, much like a standard Bag of Holding, but already filled with a random assortment of many magical colorful and pungent cheeses.

## Notes
* The bag yields infinite wedges of random cheese
* The cheese emits a stench so nauseating that drawing a piece out forces nearby creatures to gag or suffer projectile vomiting
