# King Danne II of the House Martell
## Description
Sitting on the throne of Sanctuary for 35 years, King Danne II is an elderly human ruler belonging to the royal Martell line
## Notes
* He is widely regarded across the city for his kind heart, sharp mind, and clever wit
* He governs Sanctuary alongside the Council of Seven, striving to manage the continuous influx of refugee waves while fearing rising crime, systemic court corruption, and revolutionary uprisings in the lower rings
* He relies heavily on Captain Reus Darevan and the Silverblades to maintain military defense and civil order
